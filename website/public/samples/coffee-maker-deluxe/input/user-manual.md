## Setup and Installation

### Positioning Your Coffee Maker
- Place on a flat, stable surface
- Allow at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides for ventilation
- Position near a grounded electrical outlet
- Keep away from heat sources and water splashes

### Initial Setup
1. Insert the water filter into the water tank
2. Fill the water tank to the MAX line with fresh, cold water
3. Plug in the coffee maker and press the power button
4. Follow the on-screen setup wizard to:
   - Set language preferences
   - Connect to Wi-Fi network
   - Set water hardness (use included test strip)
   - Set date and time
   - Create your first user profile

### Connecting to Wi-Fi
1. From the main menu, tap "Settings" > "Network"
2. Select your Wi-Fi network from the list
3. Enter your network password using the on-screen keyboard
4. Tap "Connect"
5. Once connected, you can download the CaféTech mobile app to control your coffee maker remotely

## Basic Operation

### Brewing a Carafe of Coffee
1. Fill the water tank with fresh, cold water (observe MIN/MAX markings)
2. Add whole beans to the bean hopper or ground coffee to the filter basket
3. Place the carafe on the warming plate
4. From the main screen, select "Carafe Brew"
5. Adjust settings if desired (strength, temperature, grind size)
6. Press "Start Brewing"

### Single-Serve Options
1. Place your cup on the drip tray
2. From the main screen, select "Single Serve"
3. Choose your desired beverage (Espresso, Americano, Cappuccino, Latte)
4. Adjust settings if desired
5. Press "Brew Now"

### Using Pre-Ground Coffee
1. Press the "Ground Coffee" button on the display
2. Open the ground coffee compartment lid
3. Add your pre-measured ground coffee (1 tbsp per cup recommended)
4. Close the lid and continue with your brewing selection

## Advanced Features

### Precision Brewing Technology
The BrewMaster Pro 5000 features advanced PID temperature control that maintains optimal brewing temperature within 1°F accuracy. This ensures perfect extraction for maximum flavor from your favorite beans.

To adjust brewing temperature:
1. From the main menu, tap "Settings" > "Brewing Parameters"
2. Select "Temperature Control"
3. Use the slider to set your desired brewing temperature (175°F-205°F)
4. Tap "Save"

### Smart Home Integration
Schedule brewing times, adjust settings, and monitor your coffee maker remotely through the companion app or voice commands.

To set up voice assistant integration:
1. Ensure your coffee maker is connected to Wi-Fi
2. Download and install the CaféTech mobile app
3. Follow the in-app instructions to connect with Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant
4. Once connected, you can use commands like:
   - "Alexa, ask CaféTech to brew coffee"
   - "Hey Google, tell CaféTech to make an espresso"

![App Interface Screenshot](placeholder-for-app-interface-image.jpg)

### Customizable Brewing Profiles
Create and save up to 10 personalized brewing profiles with your preferred settings.

To create a new profile:
1. From the main menu, tap "Profiles" > "Create New"
2. Enter a name for your profile
3. Set your preferences:
   - Grind size (1-18, fine to coarse)
   - Coffee strength (mild, medium, strong)
   - Water temperature (175°F-205°F)
   - Brew volume
   - Milk frothing level (if applicable)
4. Tap "Save Profile"

To use a saved profile:
1. From the main menu, tap "Profiles"
2. Select your desired profile
3. Tap "Brew Now"

### Scheduling Brew Times
1. From the main menu, tap "Schedule"
2. Tap "Add New Schedule"
3. Set the time, days, and brewing profile
4. Toggle "Enabled" to activate the schedule
5. Tap "Save"

## Cleaning and Maintenance

### Daily Cleaning
- Empty and rinse the drip tray
- Rinse the carafe and filter basket
- Wipe the exterior with a damp cloth

### Weekly Cleaning
- Clean the grinder using the cleaning brush
- Wash the water tank and carafe with mild soap
- Clean the milk frother (if used)

### Self-Cleaning System
The BrewMaster Pro 5000 features automated cleaning and descaling programs.

To run the cleaning cycle:
1. From the main menu, tap "Maintenance" > "Cleaning Cycle"
2. Follow the on-screen instructions
3. The cycle takes approximately 15 minutes to complete

### Descaling
Depending on water hardness and usage, descale every 3-6 months:
1. From the main menu, tap "Maintenance" > "Descaling"
2. Empty the water tank and remove the water filter
3. Fill with water and add descaling solution as directed
4. Follow the on-screen instructions
5. The complete descaling process takes approximately 30 minutes

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Coffee maker won't turn on | Not plugged in or power outage | Check power connection and circuit breaker |
| Weak coffee | Too little coffee, too coarse grind, or water too cool | Increase coffee amount, use finer grind, increase water temperature |
| Coffee overflows | Too fine grind or too much coffee | Use coarser grind or reduce coffee amount |
| Grinder not working | Bean hopper empty or foreign object in grinder | Refill beans or check for blockage |
| Error on display | Various issues | Note error code and contact customer support |
| Wi-Fi connection issues | Signal problems or incorrect password | Move closer to router or verify password |

For more troubleshooting assistance, visit cafetech.com/support or contact customer service.
