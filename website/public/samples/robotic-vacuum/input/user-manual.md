## Safety Instructions

### Important Safeguards
- Read all instructions before using the robot vacuum
- Use only as described in this manual
- Do not use outdoors or on wet surfaces
- Do not handle the charging dock or robot with wet hands
- Do not use without dust bin and filters in place
- Keep hair, loose clothing, fingers, and all parts of body away from openings and moving parts
- Do not use to pick up flammable or combustible liquids
- Do not use to pick up anything that is burning or smoking
- Do not use without the dust bin and/or filters in place
- Turn off all controls before unplugging
- Do not place objects on top of the robot
- Be extra careful when cleaning stairs
- Do not use to pick up hard or sharp objects such as glass, nails, screws, or coins
- Use extra care when cleaning drapes, tassels, or delicate items
- Keep the robot away from heated surfaces

### Child and Pet Safety
- Close supervision is necessary when used around children or pets
- Do not allow to be used as a toy
- The robot's sensors will detect and avoid most obstacles, but small pets should be supervised during operation
- Consider using no-go zones or scheduling cleaning when pets are not in the area

## Setup and Installation

### Base Station Placement
1. Place the base station on a hard, level surface against a wall
2. Ensure there is at least 1.5 feet (0.5 meters) of clearance on both sides and 4 feet (1.2 meters) in front of the base
3. Place the base station within range of a Wi-Fi signal
4. Avoid placing near stairs or drop-offs
5. Connect the power cord to the base station and plug into a wall outlet

### Robot Preparation
1. Turn the robot over and attach the side brushes by pressing them onto the mounting posts until they click
2. Turn the robot right-side up
3. Place the robot on the base station to charge
4. The robot will beep and the power indicator will pulse while charging
5. A full charge takes approximately 3 hours

## Mobile App Setup

### Downloading the App
1. Search for "SmartHome Robotics" in the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android)
2. Download and install the app
3. Create an account or log in if you already have one

### Connecting Your Robot
1. Ensure your smartphone is connected to your home Wi-Fi network (2.4GHz networks only)
2. Open the SmartHome app
3. Tap the "+" icon to add a new device
4. Select "CleanSweep Ultra AI" from the list
5. Press and hold the Power and Home buttons on the robot simultaneously for 3 seconds until you hear a beep
6. Follow the on-screen instructions to connect the robot to your Wi-Fi network
7. Once connected, you can name your robot and access all features through the app

### Initial Mapping
1. After setup, tap "Create Map" in the app
2. The robot will begin a mapping run to create a detailed floor plan of your home
3. For best results, ensure all doors are open to rooms you want mapped
4. The robot will return to the base station when mapping is complete
5. You can then customize the map by naming rooms and creating virtual boundaries

## Basic Operation

### Control Buttons
- **Power**: Press to start/pause cleaning; press and hold for 3 seconds to power on/off
- **Home**: Press to send the robot back to the base station
- **Spot Clean**: Press to start intensive cleaning of a specific area

### Starting a Cleaning Cycle
1. Press the Power button on the robot or tap "Clean" in the app
2. The robot will leave the base station and begin cleaning
3. By default, the robot will clean the entire mapped area and return to the base when finished

### Pausing and Stopping
1. Press the Power button on the robot or tap "Pause" in the app to pause cleaning
2. To resume, press the Power button again or tap "Resume" in the app
3. To end the cleaning cycle, press the Home button or tap "Dock" in the app

### Manual Control
1. In the app, tap "Manual Control"
2. Use the directional pad to steer the robot
3. Tap "Exit" to return to automatic cleaning mode

## Cleaning Modes

### Auto Clean
The default mode that intelligently cleans your entire home based on the created map.

### Room Clean
1. In the app, tap "Room Clean"
2. Select the specific rooms you want to clean
3. Tap "Start" to begin cleaning only those rooms

### Zone Clean
1. In the app, tap "Zone Clean"
2. Draw a box around the specific area you want to clean
3. Tap "Start" to begin cleaning only that zone

### Spot Clean
1. Place the robot near the soiled area
2. Press the Spot Clean button on the robot or tap "Spot Clean" in the app
3. The robot will clean in a spiral pattern, intensively cleaning a 3ft x 3ft area

### Scheduled Cleaning
1. In the app, tap "Schedule"
2. Tap "Add Schedule"
3. Select the days, time, rooms, and cleaning mode
4. Toggle the schedule on/off as needed

## Mapping and Navigation

### Multi-Floor Mapping
The CleanSweep Ultra AI can store up to 4 different floor plans.
1. In the app, tap "Maps" > "Add New Map"
2. Take the robot to the new floor and place it on the floor (not on the base station)
3. Tap "Start Mapping"
4. The robot will create a new map of the floor
5. The robot will automatically recognize which map to use based on its location

### No-Go Zones
1. In the app, tap "Maps" > select the map > "Edit"
2. Tap "No-Go Zone" and draw rectangles around areas you want the robot to avoid
3. Tap "Save" to apply changes

### Virtual Walls
1. In the app, tap "Maps" > select the map > "Edit"
2. Tap "Virtual Wall" and draw lines to create boundaries the robot won't cross
3. Tap "Save" to apply changes

### Furniture and Obstacle Detection
- The robot uses 3D sensors to detect and navigate around furniture and obstacles
- The RGB camera helps identify and avoid common household items like shoes, pet bowls, and cords
- For best results, pick up small items and secure loose cords before cleaning

## Mopping System

### Preparing for Mopping
1. Remove the water tank from the back of the robot
2. Open the water filling port and fill with clean water only (no cleaning solutions)
3. Close the port securely
4. If not already attached, snap the mopping pad onto the bottom of the water tank
5. Slide the water tank back into the robot until it clicks

### Mopping Settings
1. In the app, tap "Cleaning Preferences" > "Mopping"
2. Select the water flow level (Low, Medium, or High)
3. Enable or disable "Auto-Avoid Carpets" feature
4. For specific rooms, you can customize the water flow level in the room settings

### After Mopping
1. Remove the water tank
2. Empty any remaining water
3. Remove and rinse the mopping pad
4. Allow the mopping pad to air dry completely before reattaching
5. Clean the water tank monthly by rinsing with warm water

### Mopping Tips
- For best results, vacuum the floor before mopping
- Use only water in the tank; cleaning solutions may damage the system
- Remove the mopping attachment when not in use
- Replace mopping pads every 2-3 months depending on usage

## Self-Emptying Base Station

### Auto-Empty Function
- After each cleaning cycle, the robot will return to the base station
- The base station will automatically begin extracting dust and debris from the robot's dust bin
- This process takes approximately 15-20 seconds and may be louder than normal operation
- The dust is collected in a sealed bag inside the base station

### Dust Bag Replacement
1. Lift the top cover of the base station
2. Pull up on the dust bag handle to remove the full bag
3. The bag will automatically seal when removed
4. Insert a new dust bag, ensuring it clicks into place
5. Close the top cover

### Base Station Maintenance
- Check the dust bag monthly and replace when full
- Clean the charging contacts on both the robot and base station monthly with a dry cloth
- Check the dust extraction port for clogs if you notice reduced suction

### Cleaning the Dust Bin (Manual)
1. Press the dust bin release button and pull to remove
2. Open the dust bin lid and empty contents into a trash bin
3. Rinse the dust bin with water if necessary
4. Allow to dry completely before reinstalling
5. Note: If using the self-emptying base, the dust bin will be emptied automatically

### Cleaning the Main Brush
1. Turn the robot over
2. Press the brush guard tabs and lift to remove the cover
3. Lift out the main brush
4. Clean hair and debris from the brush and brush compartment
5. Reinstall the brush and brush guard

### Cleaning the Sensors
1. Turn the robot over
2. Wipe the cliff sensors on the bottom with a clean, dry cloth
3. Turn the robot right-side up
4. Wipe the LiDAR sensor dome with a clean, dry cloth
5. Clean the camera lens and 3D sensors on the front with a clean, dry cloth

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Robot won't turn on | Battery depleted | Place on charging dock and ensure charging contacts are clean |
| Robot won't charge | Poor connection to base | Clean charging contacts on robot and base station |
| Poor cleaning performance | Brushes tangled or dustbin full | Clean brushes and empty dustbin |
| Robot gets stuck frequently | Cluttered environment | Remove small objects from floor and use no-go zones |
| Robot can't find base station | Base station poorly positioned or blocked | Ensure base station is against wall with clear path |
| App can't connect to robot | Wi-Fi issues | Ensure robot is within range of Wi-Fi and restart router |
| Mopping leaves streaks | Dirty mopping pad or improper water flow | Replace mopping pad or adjust water flow settings |
| Base station not emptying robot | Clogged extraction port or full dust bag | Check for clogs or replace dust bag |
| Error message: "Wheels suspended" | Robot is lifted or stuck | Place robot on flat surface and remove any obstacles |
| Error message: "Brush blocked" | Main brush is tangled | Clean the main brush and remove any debris |

### Error Codes

| Code | Meaning | Solution |
|------|---------|----------|
| E001 | Wheel suspended | Place robot on flat surface |
| E002 | Bumper stuck | Tap bumper to dislodge any debris |
| E003 | Cliff sensor blocked | Clean cliff sensors |
| E004 | Low battery | Place robot on charging dock |
| E005 | Brush blocked | Clean main brush |
| E006 | Side brush blocked | Clean side brushes |
| E007 | Lidar sensor blocked | Check and clean LiDAR sensor dome |
| E008 | Dustbin missing | Install dustbin |
| E009 | Dustbin full | Empty dustbin |
| E010 | Network error | Check Wi-Fi connection |

### Factory Reset
If you experience persistent issues, perform a factory reset:
1. Ensure the robot is powered on
2. Press and hold the Power and Home buttons simultaneously for 10 seconds
3. The robot will beep and all lights will flash
4. The robot will restart and you will need to set it up again
