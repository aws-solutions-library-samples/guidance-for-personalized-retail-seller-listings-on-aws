## Setup and Pairing

### Initial Setup
1. Ensure your headphones are charged
2. Press and hold the Power/Bluetooth button for 3 seconds to turn on the headphones
3. When powering on for the first time, the headphones will automatically enter pairing mode
4. The LED indicator will flash blue rapidly to indicate pairing mode

### Pairing with a Smartphone
1. On your smartphone, go to Settings > Bluetooth and ensure Bluetooth is turned on
2. Look for "SoundSphere Pro X" in the list of available devices
3. Tap to connect
4. Once connected, the LED indicator will show a solid blue light
5. A voice prompt will confirm "Connected"

### Multipoint Connection
The SoundSphere Pro X can connect to two devices simultaneously.

To set up multipoint connection:
1. Pair with your first device as described above
2. Once connected, press and hold the Power/Bluetooth button for 5 seconds to re-enter pairing mode
3. Pair with your second device
4. Both devices are now connected to your headphones

To switch between connected devices:
1. Pause audio on the current device
2. Play audio on the second device

### Wired Connection
For high-resolution audio or when battery power is unavailable:
1. Connect the included 3.5mm audio cable to the headphone jack
2. Connect the other end to your audio source
3. The headphones will work in passive mode without power, but ANC and touch controls require battery power

## Basic Operation

### Power On/Off
- **Power On**: Press and hold the Power/Bluetooth button for 3 seconds
- **Power Off**: Press and hold the Power/Bluetooth button for 3 seconds

### Volume Control
- **Increase Volume**: Swipe up on the right ear cup touch panel
- **Decrease Volume**: Swipe down on the right ear cup touch panel

### Playback Control
- **Play/Pause**: Double-tap the right ear cup touch panel
- **Next Track**: Swipe forward on the right ear cup touch panel
- **Previous Track**: Swipe backward on the right ear cup touch panel

### Call Management
- **Answer Call**: Double-tap the right ear cup when a call is incoming
- **End Call**: Double-tap the right ear cup during a call
- **Reject Call**: Press and hold the right ear cup touch panel for 2 seconds when a call is incoming
- **Mute/Unmute**: Triple-tap the right ear cup during a call

## Active Noise Cancellation

The SoundSphere Pro X features advanced hybrid active noise cancellation with three distinct modes to suit different environments.

### ANC Modes
- **Max Mode**: Maximum noise cancellation for loud environments like airplanes or busy offices
- **Adaptive Mode**: Automatically adjusts noise cancellation level based on your environment
- **Transparency Mode**: Allows ambient sound to pass through, ideal for conversations or awareness of surroundings

### Switching Between Modes
1. Press the ANC mode button once to cycle between modes
2. A voice prompt will announce the current mode
3. Alternatively, use the AudioLux Connect app for more customization options

### Adaptive ANC Technology
The SoundSphere Pro X uses 8 strategically placed microphones to analyze and cancel ambient noise:
- External microphones detect incoming noise
- Internal microphones monitor the sound reaching your ears
- Advanced DSP algorithms create inverse sound waves to cancel noise
- Real-time adjustment based on environmental changes and fit

## Touch Controls

The right ear cup features a touch-sensitive panel for intuitive control of your audio experience.

### Touch Gestures
- **Tap twice**: Play/pause music or answer/end calls
- **Swipe forward**: Skip to next track
- **Swipe backward**: Return to previous track
- **Swipe up**: Increase volume
- **Swipe down**: Decrease volume
- **Press and hold (2 seconds)**: Activate voice assistant
- **Cover entire ear cup with palm**: Quick conversation mode (temporarily enables transparency mode and lowers volume)

### Customizing Touch Controls
Touch controls can be customized through the AudioLux Connect app:
1. Open the AudioLux Connect app
2. Go to "Device Settings" > "Controls"
3. Select the gesture you want to customize
4. Choose your preferred function from the available options
5. Tap "Save" to apply changes

## Audio Features

### Sound Profiles
The SoundSphere Pro X offers multiple sound profiles to enhance your listening experience:
- **Balanced** (default): Accurate, neutral sound reproduction
- **Bass Boost**: Enhanced low frequencies for bass-heavy music
- **Vocal Clarity**: Enhanced mid-range for podcasts and vocals
- **Spacious**: Wider soundstage for an immersive experience
- **Custom EQ**: Create your own sound profile with the 10-band equalizer in the app

To change sound profiles:
1. Open the AudioLux Connect app
2. Go to "Sound" > "Sound Profiles"
3. Select your preferred profile or create a custom one

### Hi-Res Audio
The SoundSphere Pro X is Hi-Res Audio certified, capable of reproducing frequencies up to 40kHz:
- For optimal Hi-Res Audio performance, use the included 3.5mm cable with a compatible Hi-Res Audio source
- When using Bluetooth, enable LDAC or aptX HD codec in the AudioLux Connect app for the highest wireless audio quality

### Spatial Audio
Experience immersive 360° sound with the spatial audio feature:
1. Open the AudioLux Connect app
2. Go to "Sound" > "Spatial Audio"
3. Toggle the feature on
4. Select your preferred spatial audio mode (Movie, Music, or Game)

## Battery and Charging

### Battery Life
- Up to 40 hours with ANC enabled
- Up to 60 hours with ANC disabled
- Actual battery life may vary depending on volume level and usage

### Charging Instructions
1. Connect the USB-C end of the charging cable to the headphones
2. Connect the other end to a USB power source
3. The LED indicator will glow red during charging
4. When fully charged, the LED will turn green

### Quick Charge
A 5-minute charge provides up to 5 hours of playback time when battery is low.

### Battery Status
- Check battery status via the AudioLux Connect app
- Press the Power button briefly when headphones are on to hear a voice prompt with the current battery level
- LED indicator will flash red when battery is low

## Care and Maintenance

### Cleaning
- Wipe the headphones with a soft, slightly damp cloth
- For ear cushions, use a dry cloth with a small amount of mild soap if necessary
- Allow to dry completely before use
- Do not use alcohol, solvents, or harsh cleaning agents

### Storage
1. Turn off the headphones when not in use
2. Fold the headphones as shown in the diagram below
3. Store in the provided carrying case
4. Keep in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight

![Folding Instructions](placeholder-for-folding-instructions-image.jpg)

### Ear Cushion Replacement
Ear cushions should be replaced every 12-18 months depending on usage:
1. Gently grip the edge of the ear cushion
2. Pull outward to detach from the ear cup
3. Align the new ear cushion with the ear cup
4. Press firmly around the edges until it snaps into place

Replacement ear cushions can be purchased from audiolux.com or authorized retailers.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Headphones won't turn on | Battery depleted | Charge the headphones for at least 10 minutes |
| No sound | Volume too low, incorrect source, or connection issue | Check volume, ensure correct audio source is selected, reconnect Bluetooth |
| Can't pair with device | Bluetooth issue or headphones not in pairing mode | Restart both devices, ensure headphones are in pairing mode |
| Poor sound quality | Codec issue or interference | Check codec settings in app, move away from sources of interference |
| ANC not working effectively | Poor fit or firmware issue | Ensure proper fit, update firmware via app |
| Touch controls unresponsive | System error | Restart headphones, ensure hands are dry |
| Battery draining quickly | ANC always on or high volume | Use ANC only when needed, lower volume |
| Intermittent connection | Interference or distance issue | Stay within 30ft/10m of device, avoid obstacles |

### Factory Reset
If you experience persistent issues, perform a factory reset:
1. Turn off the headphones
2. Press and hold both the Power/Bluetooth button and ANC mode button for 10 seconds
3. The LED will flash purple three times
4. The headphones will power off and reset to factory settings
5. You will need to pair your devices again

## Firmware Updates

Keeping your firmware updated ensures optimal performance and access to the latest features.

To update firmware:
1. Open the AudioLux Connect app
2. Go to "Device Settings" > "Firmware Update"
3. If an update is available, tap "Download and Install"
4. Keep the headphones connected to your device during the update
5. Do not turn off the headphones during the update process
