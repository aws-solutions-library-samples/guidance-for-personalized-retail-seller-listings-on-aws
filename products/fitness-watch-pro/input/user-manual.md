## Setup and Pairing

### Initial Setup
1. Charge your PulseTrack Elite X2 using the included magnetic charging cable
2. Press and hold the multi-function button for 3 seconds to power on
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to select your language and accept the terms of service
4. Download the FitTech Connect app on your smartphone if you haven't already

### Pairing with Your Smartphone
1. Open the FitTech Connect app on your smartphone
2. Create a new account or log in to your existing account
3. Tap "Add Device" in the app
4. Select "PulseTrack Elite X2" from the list of available devices
5. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the pairing process
6. Allow all requested permissions for full functionality

### Wi-Fi Connection
1. In the FitTech Connect app, go to "Device Settings" > "Wi-Fi"
2. Select your Wi-Fi network from the list
3. Enter your network password
4. Tap "Connect"

## Basic Operation

### Touchscreen Navigation
- **Swipe down**: Access quick settings (Do Not Disturb, Brightness, etc.)
- **Swipe up**: View notifications
- **Swipe left/right**: Cycle through widgets (Heart Rate, Weather, Activity, etc.)
- **Tap**: Select an item or open an app
- **Press and hold**: Change watch face or access additional options
- **Double-tap**: Return to the home screen from any app

### Button Functions
- **Single press**: Wake screen / Return to watch face
- **Double press**: Open favorite app (customizable)
- **Press and hold**: Power on/off or access voice assistant
- **Press and hold for 10 seconds**: Force restart (if device becomes unresponsive)

### Changing Watch Faces
1. Press and hold on the current watch face
2. Swipe left or right to browse available watch faces
3. Tap on a watch face to select it
4. Additional watch faces can be downloaded from the FitTech Connect app

## Health Monitoring Features

The PulseTrack Elite X2 offers comprehensive health monitoring with clinical-grade accuracy.

### Heart Rate Monitoring
- **Continuous monitoring**: Tracks your heart rate 24/7
- **Heart rate zones**: Shows your current zone during workouts
- **Resting heart rate**: Measured during sleep and periods of inactivity
- **Heart rate alerts**: Notifies you of unusually high or low heart rates

To view your heart rate:
1. Swipe to the Heart Rate widget on your watch
2. Tap to open the Heart Rate app for more detailed information
3. In the FitTech Connect app, go to "Health Data" > "Heart Rate" for historical data and trends

### ECG (Electrocardiogram)
- Captures electrical signals from your heart to detect irregular rhythms
- Not intended for medical diagnosis

To take an ECG reading:
1. Open the ECG app on your watch
2. Rest your arm on a flat surface
3. Place your finger on the multi-function button (which serves as the second electrode)
4. Remain still for 30 seconds while the reading is taken
5. View results on your watch and in the FitTech Connect app

### Blood Oxygen (SpO2) Monitoring
- Measures the oxygen saturation level in your blood
- Automatic measurements during sleep
- On-demand measurements any time

To take a blood oxygen reading:
1. Open the SpO2 app on your watch
2. Keep your wrist flat and still
3. Wait 15-20 seconds for the measurement to complete
4. View your results and historical data

### Sleep Tracking
- Automatically tracks your sleep duration and quality
- Identifies sleep stages (light, deep, REM)
- Provides a sleep score and recommendations

To view sleep data:
1. Open the Sleep app on your watch for last night's summary
2. For detailed analysis, check the FitTech Connect app under "Health Data" > "Sleep"

### Stress Monitoring
- Measures heart rate variability to estimate stress levels
- Provides guided breathing exercises to help reduce stress

To check stress levels:
1. Open the Stress app on your watch
2. View your current stress level and daily pattern
3. Tap "Breathe" to start a guided breathing session

To enable/disable:

To use the AI Coach:

2. Tap the coach icon in the workout screen
3. Follow the on-screen guidance and feedback

## Smart Features

### Notifications
Receive alerts for calls, texts, emails, and app notifications directly on your wrist.

To manage notifications:
1. In the FitTech Connect app, go to "Device Settings" > "Notifications"
2. Toggle on/off for each app or notification type

### Music Control
Control music playback on your connected smartphone.

To use music controls:
1. During playback on your phone, swipe right to access the music widget
2. Use the on-screen controls to play/pause, skip tracks, and adjust volume

### Contactless Payments
Make purchases directly from your wrist using NFC technology.

To set up payments:
1. In the FitTech Connect app, go to "Wallet"
2. Tap "Add Payment Card"
3. Follow the instructions to add your card
4. Set a PIN code for security

To make a payment:
1. Press the multi-function button twice to access the wallet
2. Enter your PIN if prompted
3. Hold your watch near the payment terminal

### Voice Assistant
Access your phone's voice assistant directly from your watch.

To use the voice assistant:
1. Press and hold the multi-function button for 2 seconds
2. When the assistant icon appears, speak your command
3. Release the button when finished

## Battery and Charging

### Battery Life
- Standard mode: Up to 14 days
- GPS mode: Up to 36 hours
- Always-on display: Reduces battery life by approximately 30-40%

### Charging Instructions
1. Align the charging contacts on the back of the watch with the magnetic charger
2. The watch will display the charging icon when properly connected
3. A full charge takes approximately 2 hours

### Battery Saving Tips
- Lower screen brightness
- Reduce timeout duration
- Disable always-on display
- Turn off continuous heart rate monitoring when not needed
- Use GPS only during workouts
- Enable battery saver mode for extended trips

## Care and Maintenance

### Cleaning
- Wipe the watch and band regularly with a lint-free cloth
- For deeper cleaning, use a cloth dampened with water or mild soap
- Dry thoroughly after exposure to sweat or water

### Band Replacement
1. Locate the quick-release pins on the underside of the watch
2. Slide the pin inward to release the current band
3. Align the new band with the attachment points
4. Insert until you hear a click

### Water Resistance
While the PulseTrack Elite X2 is water-resistant to 50 meters (5 ATM), please note:
- Rinse with fresh water after exposure to salt water
- Dry thoroughly before charging
- Avoid pressing buttons underwater
- Water resistance may decrease over time; avoid steam rooms and saunas

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Watch won't turn on | Battery depleted | Charge for at least 30 minutes |
| Unresponsive screen | Software crash | Press and hold button for 10 seconds to restart |
| Not syncing with phone | Bluetooth connection issue | Ensure Bluetooth is enabled, restart both devices |
| Inaccurate heart rate | Improper wear or dirty sensors | Wear watch finger-width above wrist bone, clean sensors |
| GPS signal lost | Environmental interference | Move to open area, ensure GPS is enabled |
| Battery draining quickly | Background features active | Check for active GPS, reduce brightness, disable always-on display |
| Notifications not working | App permissions issue | Check notification permissions in phone settings |

For additional troubleshooting, visit fittech.com/support or contact customer service.

## Firmware Updates

Keeping your firmware updated ensures optimal performance and access to the latest features.

To update firmware:
1. Ensure your watch is charged to at least 50%
2. Connect to Wi-Fi or keep the watch near your paired phone
3. In the FitTech Connect app, go to "Device Settings" > "System Update"
4. If an update is available, tap "Download and Install"
5. Keep the watch on the charger during the update process
